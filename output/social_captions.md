## 2. The Read Aloud Project
### Instagram
Did you know that just 15 minutes of daily reading aloud wires your child's brain for lifelong learning?

Screens stream visual data, but your voice builds neural architecture. When a child hears you read, their brain fires in sync with your tone, building vocabulary and emotional resilience simultaneously.

3 Quick Read-Aloud Tips Today:
1. Trace your finger under the words.
2. Use expressive voices to fire up auditory processing.
3. Pause to ask: 'What do you think happens next?'

Give them your voice, not a screen.
